# The Condo & BEPS Question Sheet
MBRACE INSIGHT · Max Fisher · Educational draft · Sources checked 2026-10-29

For a conversation with your board, manager, program administrator, or qualified adviser. This is not a legal or compliance determination.

## Identify the building
- Jurisdiction and address:
- Building ID / reported gross floor area / property type:
- Responsible owner, association, or manager:
- Applicable program, source URL, date checked:
- Has coverage or an exemption been confirmed by the administrator?

## Ask about the record
- Who manages annual energy benchmarking?
- Where is the latest submitted record and official correspondence?
- Which performance baseline, target, and deadline apply?
- Is a pathway or improvement plan approved, proposed, or unknown?
- What is the current capital-planning process?
- Which responsibilities concern the whole building versus an individual unit?
- What decision, if any, is required from residents now?

## Start with official sources
- DC DOEE: https://doee.dc.gov/service/building-energy-performance-standards-beps
- Maryland MDE: https://mde.maryland.gov/programs/air/ClimateChange/Clean-Buildings/Pages/BEPS.aspx
- Montgomery County adopted regulation and scope: https://www2.montgomerycountymd.gov/mcgportalapps/Press_Detail.aspx?Item_ID=46611

A performance gap alone does not prove present noncompliance, required HVAC replacement, or a funded project. Verify current coverage, deadlines, approved pathways, and administrator correspondence.
