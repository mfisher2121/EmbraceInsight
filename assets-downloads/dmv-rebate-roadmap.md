# The DMV Rebate Roadmap
MBRACE INSIGHT · Max Fisher · Educational draft · Sources checked 2026-10-29

Start with your address and utility. A program cap is not an award for your home.

## Official routes
- DC: Review the separate federal assistance and local residential rebate routes: https://doee.dc.gov/service/federal-home-energy-rebates-and-healthy-homes-act-2024
- Maryland: MEA reports updated applications submitted September 30, 2026; federal program launch depends on DOE approval: https://energy.maryland.gov/Pages/IRArebates.aspx
- Virginia: The October 13, 2026 FAQ says the federal programs are not available; launch and access details remain pending: https://energy.virginia.gov/energy-efficiency/HomeEnergyRebatesFrequentlyAskedQuestions.shtml
- Tax timing: The IRS describes the Section 25C credit for improvements through 2025. Do not assume a new 2026 installation qualifies: https://www.irs.gov/credits-deductions/energy-efficient-home-improvement-credit
