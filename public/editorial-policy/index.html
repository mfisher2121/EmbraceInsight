EDITORIAL STANDARD

The source comes with the story.

By Max Fisher · Policy draft for review · 2026-10-29

MBRACE INSIGHT is an independent educational publication for residents of Washington, DC, Maryland, and Virginia. We explain electrification, heat pumps, incentives, and building-performance issues using attributable public sources.

What insider insight means here

We read program details, explain the differences between jurisdictions, and connect those details to practical questions. We do not claim privileged government access, official agency status, or access to unannounced funding.

Sources and dates

Time-sensitive statements link to the responsible agency or program. A source-checked date records when we reviewed that information. It is not a promise that the information remains unchanged or that the page updates automatically.

Evidence and interpretation

Program status, quoted rules, and our interpretation are kept distinct. Missing information stays unknown. A program headline does not establish household eligibility; a building’s performance data does not by itself establish noncompliance.

Corrections and responsibility

Max Fisher is the responsible publisher. Send a source, a correction, or a question to mfisher@mbraceinsight.com. Material corrections should identify what changed and when. No government endorsement or installation service is claimed.

Commercial relationships

Any future paid placement, affiliate link, or referral relationship must be clearly identified where it appears. This draft does not present a paid contractor directory or endorse a provider. The separate SEO agency is outside this publication’s resident-facing offer.

Publishing quality

Semantic Gate remains our internal six-gate release requirement for the final public URL and target query. This owner-private draft is not publicly certified. It retains private access and indexing restrictions pending launch review.
