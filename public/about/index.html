About MBRACE INSIGHT

Local reporting and practical guides for Montgomery County HVAC owners. MBRACE INSIGHT is an independent publication by Max Fisher. It isn’t a government site, a contractor or a lead service.

What we publish

  • Market Reports use public records, such as county permits and Census estimates, to show what is happening in specific ZIP codes. Each one lists its sources, retrieval dates and limits.
  • Contractor Playbook guides take a decision owners face on real calls and lay out a simple way to explain it to a homeowner.

What we don’t publish

No rankings, no lead lists and no comparisons between named companies. No lead costs or close rates until we have data we can cite. When a question can’t be answered from the record, we say so.

How we work

Official sources, linked. Observations, calculations and estimates labeled. Retrieval dates visible. Unknowns stay unknown. The full editorial standard explains how we handle sources and corrections.

Contact

Corrections, data and story ideas: mfisher@mbraceinsight.com. There’s no email list yet; when there is, signing up will be a separate, clear choice.
