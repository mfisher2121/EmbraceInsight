A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Know what
you’re signing up for.

Does the form run an instant automated check?+

No. It saves a page-clearance review request with your URL and target query. The evidence must be collected and assessed before a real verdict can be issued. The sample report is an example of the format.

What do the six gates cover?+

G0 Crawlability, G1 Index Eligibility, G2 Query Alignment, G3 Semantic Coverage, G4 Citation Map, and G5 Trust Signals. A full PASS requires evidence for every condition; missing evidence fails the relevant gate.

Does getting a resource subscribe me to the newsletter?+

Only if you choose the separate, unchecked newsletter option. A resource request and a newsletter request are recorded distinctly. You can withdraw your newsletter request using the preference link shown after signup.

Will I receive an email immediately?+

This private preview records requests and unlocks resource downloads immediately. It does not send a report, a confirmation email, or a newsletter automatically. Newsletter requests remain pending email confirmation until the delivery workflow is connected.

Is a PASS a promise that my page will rank?+

No. The method assesses structural eligibility for a specified query. Indexing and rankings are not guaranteed. Google’s technical requirements also distinguish eligibility from guaranteed indexing.

Can I start without requesting a full review?+

Yes. Choose a checklist or worksheet in the resource library, or join the newsletter. Those paths do not require a page URL or a review request.
