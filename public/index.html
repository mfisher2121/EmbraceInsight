A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Clear context.
Clear expectations.

Can you tell me which rebate I qualify for?+

Our guides help you find the responsible program and prepare questions. The administrator determines eligibility. Program status, utility, income, equipment, contractor rules, and timing can change the answer. We do not promise approval or a rebate amount.

Does BEPS mean every homeowner must replace their HVAC?+

No. Start by checking whether a particular building is covered under the applicable jurisdiction’s program. A threshold or performance gap alone does not establish a replacement obligation. Condo boards and building managers should confirm the building’s record with the administrator.

Is this a government or utility website?+

MBRACE INSIGHT is an independent educational publication. We link to official agencies and programs so you can verify information directly. We do not claim government endorsement, privileged access, or authority to approve applications.
